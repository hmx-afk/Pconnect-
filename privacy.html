Privacy Policy

PConnect

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PConnect collects, uses, stores and shares information when people use our service marketplace.

PConnect currently operates on the Pi Network Testnet. Test transactions are for development and testing only. They are not Mainnet payments.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PConnect, we may collect:

  • Your Pi Network username and account identification information used for authentication.
  • Your name and contact information submitted with a service request.
  • Service category and project details you provide.
  • Request information, including request status and timestamps.
  • Payment-related information such as payment identifiers, transaction IDs, amounts and statuses.
  • Notifications and related account activity within PConnect.
  • Temporary browser session information needed to operate the application and manage requests or payment flows.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users through Pi Network.
  • Receive, review and manage service requests.
  • Connect customers with appropriate service providers.
  • Verify and manage Testnet payment transactions.
  • Display request updates and notifications.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve PConnect.
  • Protect the platform against misuse and unauthorized activity.

3. When Information Is Shared

PConnect is a marketplace that connects customers with service providers. An administrator first reviews a submitted request. If the request is approved and a service provider is selected, only information reasonably needed to discuss or carry out the approved service may be shared with that selected provider.

This may include the customer's name, contact information and relevant project details. We do not intend to make these customer details publicly available to all service providers or other marketplace users.

Information may also be processed by technology providers supporting PConnect, including Pi Network for authentication and payment verification, Supabase for backend data services, and Vercel for application hosting.

4. Pi Network Authentication and Payments

PConnect uses Pi Network services for user authentication and Testnet payment-related operations. Information required to verify identity or payment status may be exchanged with Pi Network services.

PConnect does not treat a Testnet transaction as a real monetary payment or as a Mainnet transaction.

5. Storage and Security

Information submitted through PConnect may be stored in the platform's backend systems so that requests, notifications and transaction records can be managed.

The application also uses temporary browser session storage for certain session and request-related information. We apply technical and administrative measures intended to protect information, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is needed to operate the marketplace, manage requests and transactions, maintain records, address support issues or meet applicable operational requirements. Actual retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and its purpose.

7. Your Choices and Responsibilities

Please provide accurate information and avoid submitting sensitive information that is not necessary for your service request. You should only provide contact details that you are comfortable sharing with the selected service provider when necessary to carry out an approved service.

For questions about your information or a privacy concern, contact our support team using the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

PConnect is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the platform, please contact our support team.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as PConnect develops. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

10. Contact Support

For privacy questions or support, contact: supportpconnect@gmail.com
